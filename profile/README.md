# NextFerret : The Arvor Project Division

## [ENGLISH]

Welcome to The Arvor Division of NextFerret, formally recognized as The Arvor Project. 
We are the dedicated developers, architects, and maintainers behind Arvor Linux and 
its comprehensive ecosystem of core system utilities.

Our paramount mission is to architect and deliver a modern, high-performance Linux 
ecosystem, propelled by powerful, specialized, and meticulously engineered utilities 
designed to elevate the user experience and system administration to unprecedented 
levels.

Our core initiatives include:

• Arvor Linux: An atomic distribution engineered with a paradigm that eschews forced 
  immutability, thereby granting users absolute sovereignty and granular control over 
  their operating environment.

• napt: An advanced, transactional package manager meticulously crafted to ensure 
  safe, reliable, and verifiable system updates without compromising system integrity.

• nlc: A robust and highly streamlined chroot manager, facilitating the seamless 
  creation and management of secure, isolated environments for diverse operational needs.

• nsm: A powerful, state-of-the-art snapshot manager explicitly built for seamless 
  system recovery, version control, and comprehensive state management.

We cordially invite you to explore our repositories, engage with our ongoing projects, 
and join us in shaping the definitive future of Arvor Linux.

Join our community on Discord: https://discord.gg/PmCMYzYEKr
